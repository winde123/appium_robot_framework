# QR codes at land checkpoints

Last reviewed: 2026-09-07

QR onboarding, manual profile creation, an individual QR, and car, motorcycle, lorry and bus group journeys, including group editing, deletion and the foreign visitor SGAC reminder.

[Word walkthrough](04-qr-codes-at-land-checkpoints.docx)

| Flow | Observed outcome |
| --- | --- |
| QR tutorial | Welcome and all seven tutorial steps captured through Finish. |
| Individual resident profile and QR | Completed: WALKTHROUGH QR RESIDENT saved as the own profile and an individual QR generated. |
| Email validation | Required email feedback captured before completing the profile. |
| Car group | A two-person car group saved, including the foreign visitor. Generation displayed an SGAC reminder and its handoff was followed. |
| Motorcycle group | Two-person group and QR generated. The form displays a two-person maximum. |
| Lorry group | Two-person group and QR generated. The form displays a four-person maximum. |
| Bus group | Two-person group and QR generated. The form displays a four-person maximum. |
| Manage group | Bus group renamed to WALKTHROUGH BUS EDITED, regenerated and deleted after confirmation. |
| Checkpoint use | QR presentation screens reached; actual immigration clearance requires checkpoint hardware and was not performed. |
